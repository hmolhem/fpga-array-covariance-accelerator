\chapter{Project Definition and Engineering Requirements}
\label{ch:project-definition}

\section{Engineering Problem}
The EE 6800 project addresses one controlled engineering question: how can an existing
floating-point sample-covariance-matrix (SCM) front end be translated into a traceable
fixed-point implementation on the PYNQ-Z2 while preserving numerical meaning and
producing independently reviewable software, HLS, implementation, interface, board, and
downstream direction-of-arrival (DOA) evidence?

The project is intentionally organized as an evidence chain rather than as a collection of
unconnected demonstrations:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\text{Requirement} &\rightarrow \text{Theory} \rightarrow \text{Reference} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Numerical Design} \rightarrow \text{FPGA Implementation} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Verification} \rightarrow \text{Engineering Interpretation}
\end{aligned}}
\end{equation}

\section{Baseline Scope}
The baseline is deliberately narrow, consistent with the approved proposal~\cite{molhem2026ee6800proposal}: one ULA reference case, $N=8$ complex channels,
$M=128$ snapshots, SCM formation in programmable logic, and host-side MUSIC or sector
estimation after the covariance matrix is returned. Controlled synthetic vectors are used to
isolate the digital implementation from RF acquisition uncertainties.

\section{Explicit Non-Goals}
The baseline does not require a new DOA algorithm, sparse-array processing, a complete
FMCW range--Doppler chain, RF/ADC acquisition, CST modeling, full MUSIC acceleration,
robotics, machine learning, or product-level power optimization. These are future extensions
only after the core evidence package is stable.

\section{Gate Closure Standard}
Every gate shall contain the same engineering record: (1) purpose, (2) scientific or
engineering question, (3) frozen inputs and assumptions, (4) theory/design basis,
(5) implementation or evaluation method, (6) verification and acceptance criteria,
(7) evidence produced, (8) engineering interpretation, and (9) explicit gate decision.
This standard is used in the report and will also govern repository pull requests.
